YOUR COACH

Laura Harvistola

My goal is not only to help you achieve results, but also to teach you the skills and habits you need to continue using what you've learned independently after coaching. I want to help you make changes that genuinely last – without endless restriction, burning yourself out or taking things to extremes.

I have been coaching since 2022. I am a certified international Personal Trainer (Intensive PT), a Gym and Strength Coach (Spartan Academy), and I have also studied nutritional sciences at the University of Helsinki.

My interest in learning more about training and nutrition began in 2018 when I started my first fitness coaching programme. I competed in Bikini Fitness from 2019 to 2025. For now, I have left competing behind, and these days training is still a big part of my life, balanced with everyday life and time spent with my husband and our little dog.

As a coach, I am supportive but I will also challenge you when needed. Change always requires change, but it doesn't have to mean giving up everything you enjoy, following endless lists of restrictions or pushing yourself to exhaustion. I will give you the tools you need to succeed and support you throughout the process. My coaching services are suitable for all training levels. Get in touch and let's talk about your goals!
