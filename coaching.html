Find the right coaching for you

Coaching isn't about being perfect. It's about finding an approach that works for you, fits your everyday life and helps you move towards your goals. Choose the option below based on whether you want support with training, nutrition or both.

TRAINING + NUTRITION

Online Coaching

149 € / month

Online Coaching is the most comprehensive option and is built around your individual goals and lifestyle. Whether your goal is fat loss, building muscle, improving your energy levels or making a lasting lifestyle change, your training and nutrition are designed to work together and fit into your everyday life – without unnecessary extremes.

You won't be left alone with a plan. We review your progress every week and adapt your coaching along the way based on your needs. Your training programme is monitored weekly, we set goals for the week ahead together, and make changes to both training and nutrition whenever needed. This means your coaching evolves with your progress rather than being a plan that's created once and then forgotten.

  • Personalised nutrition plan
  • Personalised training programme
  • Ongoing support from your coach
  • Weekly check-ins and progress tracking
  • Updates and adjustments to your training and nutrition plans
  • 3-month minimum coaching commitment

TRAINING

Online Training Coaching

119 € / month

Training Coaching is for you if you want to train with purpose, follow a programme designed specifically around your goals and have the support of a coach throughout the process.

We review your training progress every week, set goals together for the week ahead and adjust your programme as you develop. I take care of the planning and progressive programming so that you can focus on what matters – training and making progress.

  • Personalised training programme designed around your goals
  • Ongoing support from your coach
  • Weekly check-ins and progress tracking
  • Updates and adjustments to your training programme
  • 3-month minimum coaching commitment

A nutrition plan is not included in Training Coaching. If you would like nutrition coaching alongside your training, choose Online Coaching.
